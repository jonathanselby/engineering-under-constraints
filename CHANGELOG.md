# Changelog

## 0.1.0

Initial research release.

- explicit engineering-not-politics framing;
- documented research methodology and source hierarchy;
- Commodore 64 engineering lessons and worked modern-software example;
- historical studies covering Israel, United Kingdom, France, Sweden, smaller US services, Soviet/Eastern Bloc systems and NASA/Apollo;
- cross-study synthesis into modern software principles;
- reusable constraint-review method;
- central source register with primary and institutional references;
- explicit limitations in every historical study to avoid hero narratives or unsupported transfer of lessons.
